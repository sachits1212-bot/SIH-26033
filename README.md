## Problem Title
SIH 26033: Multiple intermediaries reduce farmers earnings and increase consumer prices.

## Problem Creater's Organization
Ministry of Consumer Affairs, Food & Public Distribution

## Theme
Agriculture, FoodTech & Rural Development

## Problem Description
Expected Solution: Create a digital marketplace that:

• Connects farmers/FPOs directly with consumers and bulk buyers.
• Provides logistics support.
• Uses AI for demand forecasting and route optimization.

Benefits:

• Better prices for farmers.
• Lower prices for consumers.
• Reduced supply chain inefficiencies.

## Proposed Solution:

We propose an AI-powered digital marketplace that connects farmers and Farmer Producer Organizations (FPOs) directly with consumers, retailers, and bulk buyers.

1.Farmers can register and upload their available products.
2.Buyers can directly purchase fresh agricultural products.
3.The platform eliminates unnecessary middlemen.
4.Farmers can get fair prices for their products.
5.Consumers can purchase products at affordable prices.
6.AI helps predict market demand and suggest suitable prices.
7.A tracking system helps monitor orders and deliveries.

## Technical Approach:

Technologies Used:
1.Frontend: HTML, CSS, JavaScript, React.js
2.Backend: Python, Django
3.Database: MySQL
4.AI/ML: Python, Machine Learning
5.Maps: Google Maps API
6.Payment: UPI and payment gateway
Implementation Process:
1. Farmer registration and login.
2. Upload agricultural products and prices.
3. Buyers search and select products.
4. Place orders through the platform.
5. AI predicts demand and recommends prices.
6. Logistics system finds suitable delivery routes.
7. Products are delivered directly to buyers.

## Feasibility and Viability:

Feasibility:
1.The platform can be developed using existing web technologies.
2.Farmers can access it through smartphones.
3.Digital payments make transactions easier.
4.AI can improve demand prediction and delivery planning.

Challenges:
1.Lack of internet access in rural areas.
2.Farmers may have limited technical knowledge.
3.Transportation and storage difficulties.
4.Maintaining product quality.

Solutions:
1.Provide a simple and user-friendly interface.
2.Support local languages.
3.Partner with transport companies.
4.Introduce quality verification and product tracking.
